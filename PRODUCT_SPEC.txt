1. APPLICATION OVERVIEW & IDENTITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1 Official names

  In-app and Arabic launcher name
      القرآن الكريم

  English launcher / store fallback
      Holy Quran

  Long store title (Arabic)
      القرآن الكريم كاملاً - استماع وتلاوة

  Long store title (English)
      Holy Quran - Audio & Recitation

  The interface itself is Arabic-only. Numerals in chrome, indexes, page
  pickers, and counts are rendered as Eastern Arabic digits. Layout direction
  is right-to-left from the first painted frame.

1.2 Branding intent

  The product presents itself as a complete, trustworthy digital mushaf — not
  as a social reader, not as a study dashboard, and not as a multi-religion
  toolkit. Visual identity is a circular Arabic calligraphic emblem of
  «القرآن الكريم» on a pure black field, with cyan / teal diacritic accents on
  the launcher mark. The launch image carries the same emblem plus the line
  «تلاوة و استماع», stating the two equal pillars of the product: recitation
  (reading the printed Medina page) and listening (full-surah and verse audio).

  Status-bar and media-notification marks are white stencil calligraphy on a
  transparent field so they remain legible on both light and dark system bars.

1.3 Core vision

  Deliver a printed-mushaf-faithful 604-page Medina page experience, fully
  usable offline for reading, with optional high-quality recitation that
  continues when the device is locked, in the background, or connected to a
  vehicle head unit. Every interaction — page turn, word tap, index jump,
  verse share — must feel native, immediate, and reverent.

  The work is offered as an act of worship and a gift to the Muslim community.
  The in-app “About” statement frames the product as open for reuse in service
  of the Qur’an, and invites du‘a. Contact for suggestions:
  m.s.albarho@gmail.com

1.4 Target audience

  Primary
      Arabic-reading Muslims who want a daily mushaf on a phone: accurate
      page layout, tajweed-colored glyphs, and recitation without accounts,
      feeds, or English UI chrome.

  Secondary
      Drivers using a vehicle media screen who need large, glanceable surah
      and reciter lists and simple playback controls.

  Tertiary
      Learners who long-press a word for meaning, tafsir, word audio, verse
      audio, bookmarking, and share-as-image / share-as-video.

  Device class assumed in quality targets: contemporary Android phones
  (including mid-range 6.7" class such as Galaxy A72), portrait-first, with
  support for light and dark system appearance.

1.5 Design philosophy

  Dark royal aesthetic
      Launch, adaptive icon field, and media-session chrome sit on #000000.
      Ornament is restrained: eight-pointed star / girih geometry, thin gold
      or theme-tinted frames, never busy wallpaper behind the page itself.

  High legibility
      Body UI type is a classical Arabic naskh family (Amiri). Mushaf pages
      never substitute a generic Arabic font for the page glyphs. Contrast
      between glyph, ayah marker, and paper tone is maintained across eight
      paper palettes and an independent night mode.

  Clean spatial arrangement
      A single content column, generous but not wasteful gutters, a curved
      five-slot bottom navigation, and a compact page header that appears
      only when chrome is revealed. No persistent hamburger, no stacked
      toolbars, no competing side drawers during reading.

  RTL-first
      Reading order, swipe direction, index lists, audio pickers, and
      automotive browse trees assume Arabic. Page 1 is the first Medina
      page; a swipe toward the leading edge (visually right in RTL) moves
      toward later pages in the printed order, matching physical mushaf
      muscle memory.

2. VISUAL DESIGN & INTERFACE SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 Visual system

  Launch / icon field
      Background           #000000
      Emblem               circular calligraphy, cyan–teal accents
      Safe zone            adaptive launcher art is scaled so every opaque
                           pixel of the emblem lies inside the central 66 dp
                           circle of a 108 dp layer, so OEM masks never clip
                           the calligraphy tips.

  Reading paper palettes (user-selectable, apply to mushaf chrome and page
  body; independent of night mode)

      أبيض ناصع   White
      بيج         Beige  (default)
      أزرق        Blue
      أخضر        Green
      بنفسجي      Purple
      وردي        Rose
      فيروزي      Teal
      عنبري       Amber

  Night mode
      Inverts paper to a deep charcoal family while keeping glyph contrast.
      Surah-name color glyphs in lists and the audio title are inverted to
      white so they remain readable on dark cards.

  Ornament
      Header bars, page-number discs, surah name frames, hizb / sajdah
      badges, bottom navigation, and audio player shells share a geometric
      frame language (star tile, multiply-blended chrome fill). Audio player
      and verse playback bar may carry a subtle repeating girih field.

  Iconography
      Custom Quran mark for the reading tab; list, bookmark, and settings
      marks in the remaining slots. Reciter portraits exist as cropped
      circular stills for automotive artwork. Each of the 114 surahs has a
      dedicated calligraphic still for vehicle browse cards.

2.2 Instant launch and seamless splash

  Goal
      From launcher tap to the first fully painted mushaf page, the user
      must never see a black empty window, a default application icon in the
      center of the screen, a spinner, or a “loading…” sentence.

  Cold-start sequence

      1. System launch surface
         On modern Android (API 31+), the operating-system splash uses the
         same black field and the same full splash artwork as the activity
         window. Exit of that system splash is instantaneous (no fade to
         white or to an empty activity).

      2. Activity window
         The window background is the full-bleed launch image
         («القرآن الكريم / تلاوة و استماع» on black), cropped to fill.

      3. First web paint
         Before application styles apply, the document background is
         #000000. A static full-screen image identical to the native splash
         covers the viewport at the maximum stacking level.

      4. Hydration gate
         The overlay is held until:
             - the word-level mushaf database is open and page metadata is
               available, and
             - the QCF4 page typeface for the restored page (or page 1) is
               loaded and the first page panel can paint without glyph swap.
         Database failure removes the overlay so an error and retry control
         can appear. A safety timeout (approximately eight seconds) prevents
         a stuck overlay.

      5. Handoff
         Native splash auto-hide duration is zero; there is no spinner.
         When the gate opens, overlays are removed with zero fade. The user
         should perceive a single continuous black splash that becomes the
         mushaf.

  Warm start
      Last page number and last primary tab (reading vs listening) are
      restored from local preferences so the splash hands off onto the
      same place the user left.

2.3 Screen views and layouts

  2.3.1 Shell

      Two primary tabs, five bottom-bar actions:

          الفهرس          Index (surahs / juz / hizb)
          القرآن الكريم   Reading (Medina pages)
          الاستماع        Listening (surah audio)
          آخر موضع        Saved reading positions
          الإعدادات       Settings

      The bar is a shallow curved capsule. The active item is emphasized
      with a sliding bubble. A tap on empty page area toggles chrome
      (header + bottom bar) together: both slide and fade. On the listening
      tab the bar stays visible. Header is fixed to the top of the screen
      and does not scroll with ayah lines.

  2.3.2 Tajweed mushaf page view (default: صفحات مصحف المدينة)

      Full-bleed digital facsimile of the King Fahd Complex printed page:

          - 604 pages, typical 15 lines.
          - Line types: surah name band, basmalah, ayah line, empty.
          - Each visible word is a private-use ligature from the QCF4 Hafs
            bundle mapped to that page, preserving tajweed coloring encoded
            in the typeface.
          - 47 Hafs face files plus a dedicated basmalah face are mapped so
            consecutive pages that share a face reuse the same loaded file.
          - Surah openings use a framed name ligature (QCF full-surah color
            face) with revelation place (مكية / مدنية) and ayah count
            (عدد الآيات N) as readable meta, not as part of the glyph.
          - Quarter-hizb badges (ربع، نصف، ثلاثة أرباع، بداية الحزب) and
            sajdah marks sit in the printed positions.
          - Page number sits in a circular disc; juz name uses a matching
            decorative face.

      Adjacent pages (previous / current / next) are composed in a
      horizontal track so a flip never waits on a blank panel.

  2.3.3 Text mushaf (المصحف النصي)

      Same pagination and word identity, but lines reflow as continuous
      readable text using a Uthmanic Hafs face. User-controlled size from
      20 px to 34 px in 0.5 px steps (default 25 px). Size control is
      disabled while Medina pages are selected, with an explanatory hint.

  2.3.4 Listening screen

      Reciter grid, surah list with the same QCF surah-name ligatures as
      the index, download range controls, repeat mode, and a full-screen
      player. Player title is the surah ligature with enough vertical
      measure that diacritics are not clipped. A compact restore bar
      appears on the reading tab if a surah is still playing.

  2.3.5 Overlays (modals / sheets)

      Index, page jump, ayah search, bookmarks, settings, about, word
      sheet, meaning popover, tafsir sheet, verse playback bar, reciter
      pickers, onboarding, and first-read coach mark. All are Arabic,
      dismissible by backdrop tap or the close label «إغلاق».

3. CORE FEATURES & FUNCTIONAL SPECIFICATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 Quran page viewer engine

  Pagination
      Strict 1…604 Medina pagination. Invalid stored pages are clamped.
      Jump via header page disc, numeric page picker (with range hint),
      index (surah start page, juz, hizb), search result, or bookmark.

  Horizontal flipping
      Pointer-driven horizontal panning of a three-panel track.
      Commit threshold approximately 50 px; drag begins after 8 px of
      horizontal motion. Slide settle approximately 280 ms.
      First/last page apply edge resistance rather than looping.
      Composition is hardware-accelerated; panels keep a stable width so
      line wrapping does not shift during the gesture (zero layout shift).

  Vertical rubber-banding
      The reading viewport does not bounce or stretch vertically. Vertical
      motion is ignored for paging. If the user starts a vertical drag, the
      horizontal page gesture is cancelled so the system does not fight the
      finger. The page is a fitted panel, not a scrolling document, in
      Medina mode.

  Offline rendering
      All 604 pages, word glyphs, meanings index, tafsir store, and typeface
      files ship inside the application package. No network is required to
      open a page, search text, or read tafsir. Word audio prefers a local
      file and falls back to a remote word-by-word source when absent.

  Word-level mapping
      Every painted token is a word record: identifier, surah, ayah, word
      index, QCF ligature (display), and Uthmanic Hafs string (search,
      bookmark snippet, share, audio titles). Ayah-end markers are distinct
      tokens. Unicode private-use code points in the ligature field map
      one-to-one onto the loaded page face so alignment matches the printed
      line.

  Prefetch
      Current page face loads first; previous and next faces preload. A
      small least-recently-used cache of page faces (about twelve) is kept
      so memory stays bounded on mid-range devices.

3.2 Word, verse, and study interactions

  Short tap on a word that has a gloss
      Meaning popover anchored to the word.

  Long press (about 450 ms; cancelled if the finger moves more than 10 px)
      Word sheet: location (surah / ayah / word index), word audio with
      previous/next ayah, save ayah as a reading position, play/pause the
      full verse with reciter choice, tafsir, share as still image, share
      as video (range of at most 10 consecutive ayahs, reciter required).

  Tafsir
      Loaded on demand from a separate packaged commentary store and
      released when the sheet closes to keep memory small.

  Search
      Full-text over Hafs strings, or a reference of the form «سورة:آية»
      (Western or Eastern digits). Results show snippet, surah, ayah, and
      page, and jump the viewer with a highlight.

3.3 Audio and background playback engine

  Two recitation domains

      Surah recitation (listening tab)
          Full-surah files from the selected reciter’s mushaf server.
          Play, pause, stop, seek, previous/next surah, repeat off / repeat
          one surah / play all remaining surahs in order.
          Optional download of a surah range into on-device cache; cached
          playback is labeled «يُشغَّل من التخزين المحلي».
          Reciter catalog is packaged with the application.

      Verse recitation (from the word sheet)
          Per-ayah reciters (separate catalog). Previous/next ayah while
          the verse bar is open. Word-level pronunciation uses the word
          audio sources described above.

  Continuity
      A foreground media session keeps audio alive when the screen is off,
      another application is in front, or the user leaves the reading tab.
      Lock-screen and shade controls expose play/pause/skip. Audio focus is
      requested and abandoned correctly so navigation prompts and calls
      interrupt recitation politely.

  Errors
      Distinct copy for offline-without-cache, missing file, and generic
      play failure — never a blank player.

3.4 Automotive / external display (Android Auto)

  Headless playback
      Vehicle browse and playback do not depend on the phone UI being
      visible. Audio is prepared and played by the device’s native media
      session so driving use remains reliable if the phone activity is
      sent to the background.

  Browse tree (Arabic labels, RTL)

      Root
          القراء     reciters → reciter → list of 114 surahs
          السور      flat list of 114 playable surahs

  Controls optimized for driving
      Play, pause, skip to adjacent surah, and selection from the two
      trees. Artwork: reciter stills and per-surah calligraphic cards.
      No reading of the mushaf page, no word sheets, no settings — glance
      and control only.

  Wake policy
      If the phone activity is started only to satisfy the vehicle
      connection, it is moved to the background after a short delay so the
      car screen remains the user’s focus.

3.5 Sharing

  Still image
      Composed ayah card: surah ligature, Arabic text, optional basmalah,
      branded layout, handed to the system share sheet.

  Video
      Timed recitation over a composed card, ayah range clamped to ten
      verses, duration expected under five minutes. User is warned to wait
      during encode. Cancellation of the system share sheet is not treated
      as an error.

3.6 First-run experience

  Six-step onboarding (skippable after completion, stored locally):

      1. Welcome to the Medina 604-page mushaf.
      2. Horizontal swipe; tap to reveal chrome; tap page number to jump.
      3. Printed-page fidelity: centered page numbers, quarter-hizb marks.
      4. Listening tab for full surahs; long-press a word to play a verse.
      5. Meanings, tafsir, word/verse audio, save, share.
      6. Download surahs for offline listening.

  After onboarding, a one-time coach mark on the page: long-press words.

3.7 Permissions

  Network for streaming and downloads; notifications for media; wake lock
  and foreground media playback for background audio. Onboarding’s final
  step requests what the platform requires. Failure to grant does not
  block reading.

4. USER EXPERIENCE (UX) & INTERACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1 Touch gestures

  Horizontal pan          Turn pages (see 3.1).
  Vertical axis           Does not page; cancels an in-progress horizontal
                          page drag so there is no diagonal “smear.”
  Single tap on page      Toggle header + bottom navigation.
  Long-press word         Word sheet.
  Short tap glossed word  Meaning popover.
  Long-press surah frame  Open surah index.
  Long-press juz / hizb   Open divisions index.
  Drag on player / verse  Reposition floating control bars.
  bar handle

  Interactive chrome (pickers, sheets, buttons) is excluded from page-swipe
  hit testing.

4.2 Instant app state and local caching

  Restored on launch
      Last Medina page, last primary tab, color palette, night/day, Medina
      vs text mode, text-mode font size, onboarding and coach-mark flags.

  Reading positions
      Multiple ayah bookmarks with Hafs snippet, surah, ayah, page. Created
      from the word sheet; listed under «آخر موضع»; jump or delete.

  Audio
      Cached surah files remain until the user replaces storage. Repeat
      mode and last reciter persist for the listening session.

  Databases
      Packaged mushaf, tafsir, and marker stores are copied into local
      application storage on first run and version-stamped so a later
      package can replace them without corrupting user bookmarks.

4.3 Settings and customization

  لون التطبيق          Eight paper palettes (section 2.1).
  نوع عرض الصفحات      Medina pages vs text mushaf.
  حجم خط المصحف        20–34 px, text mushaf only.
  الوضع الليلي/النهاري Toggle in the page header.
  حول التطبيق          Dedication, reuse invitation, contact.

  No language switcher: the product is Arabic-only by design.

4.4 Motion and feedback

  Page settle 280 ms; overlay open uses a single frame to attach then
  transitions in; chrome show/hide is a coordinated slide+fade. No
  gratuitous parallax. Errors are inline Arabic sentences with retry
  where recovery is possible.

4.5 Accessibility notes

  Icon-only controls have Arabic names. Ligature-only titles expose a
  textual aria-label (surah name). Dialogs are modal with explicit close.
  The product does not yet claim full talkback reading of QCF ligature
  pages; Hafs strings back search and bookmarks for that reason.

5. DATABASE & ASSET DATA ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.1 Packaged data stores

  Primary mushaf store (word-level)
      Tables conceptually include:

          words
              word identity, surah, ayah, position in ayah,
              Uthmanic Hafs UTF-8, QCF ligature, ayah-marker flag.
              Indexed by word id and by (surah, ayah).

          mushaf_pages
              page number, line number, line type (surah_name, basmallah,
              ayah, empty), centering flag, first/last word id spanning
              the line, optional surah id on the line.
              Indexed by page and by word range so a page is a contiguous
              slice of the word stream.

          surahs
              1–114, Arabic name, ayah count, revelation type
              (Meccan / Medinan), used with a derived start page.

          divisions
              juz (30), hizb (60), and related quarter metadata:
              start/end surah–ayah, labels, optional page hints.

          page_meta
              page → juz number, hizb number (fast header lookup).

          mushaf_editions
              edition identity (Medina 15-line, 604 pages).

  Marker store (separate, small)
      Quarter-hizb (rub) rows: first verse key, printed Arabic label
      (بداية الحزب، ربع الحزب، نصف الحزب، ثلاثة أرباع الحزب).
      Sajdah rows: verse key, obligatory vs recommended.
      Joined to pages through the word/page map so badges sit on the
      correct line.

  Tafsir store (separate, loaded lazily)
      Per-ayah commentary. Opened when the user requests tafsir; closed
      when the sheet is dismissed.

5.2 Derived and packaged sidecars

  QCF4 face map
      Page 1–604 → one of 47 Hafs face identifiers (plus a basmalah face).
      Consecutive pages share a face, so the loader requests files, not
      604 unique faces.

  Surah-name ligature maps
      Code point tables for the page-header / list surah face.

  Juz-name ligature map
      Decorative juz labels in the header.

  Reciter catalogs
      Full-surah reciters (listening + automotive) and per-ayah reciters
      (verse bar), including stream bases and which surahs each mushaf
      covers.

  Meanings
      Gloss strings attached to words that have them; indexed after the
      primary store is ready.

  Search index
      Built in the background over Hafs text for prefix and phrase query.

5.3 Vectorized typography and asset management

  Page faces (QCF4 Hafs)
      Compact webfont files, one per shared face, loaded on demand,
      cached with a hard cap, trimmed when the user moves far away.
      Ligatures occupy a private-use Unicode range reserved for this
      mushaf; the renderer never synthesizes missing glyphs from a
      fallback Arabic face on Medina pages.

  UI faces
      Amiri regular/bold for chrome.
      Uthmanic Hafs for text mushaf, search snippets, audio fallback
      titles, and bookmark excerpts.
      Surah-name color face for frames, index cards, audio title.
      Juz decorative face for header juz.

  Imagery
      Launch still, adaptive launcher layers, notification stencil,
      114 surah stills for the vehicle, reciter portraits, geometric
      frames drawn as resolution-independent paths rather than heavy
      bitmaps in scrolling lists.

  Audio
      Optional on-device surah cache keyed by reciter and surah.
      Word audio: local package first, remote word-by-word second.

  Memory posture
      One primary store in memory for reading; tafsir not resident until
      asked; page faces LRU; marker store is tiny; vehicle playback uses
      the same packaged reciter list and surah metadata without opening
      the reading renderer.

5.4 Integrity and versioning

  Mushaf package version and tafsir package version are independent.
  Installing a newer application replaces packaged stores when the
  version stamp changes. User bookmarks, preferences, and downloaded
  surah files survive across those replacements.

  Page construction is a join: page lines → word id range → ligature +
  Hafs + meaning. Header juz/hizb comes from page_meta with a
  deterministic fallback from page number if meta is missing. A small
  set of known printed-page surah-boundary overrides keeps index labels
  aligned with the physical mushaf.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. NON-GOALS (CURRENT PRODUCT BOUNDARY)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Multi-language UI (English chrome is not offered).
  - User accounts, cloud sync, or social features.
  - Translation-as-primary-reading-mode (meanings and tafsir are
    supporting, not a parallel mushaf).
  - Editing or annotating the rasm.
  - Video recitation inside the reader (share-as-video is an export).
  - Non-Hafs riwayat as a page renderer (audio mushafs may vary; the
    painted page is Hafs Medina).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. QUALITY BAR (EXPERIENCE ACCEPTANCE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Cold start from the launcher icon shows the branded black still until
  the mushaf page is paintable; no centered default icon, no empty black
  flash, no loading sentence.

  A full swipe between pages does not reflow lines, does not bounce
  vertically, and does not flash a missing typeface.

  Background surah audio survives screen-off and app-switch; vehicle
  browse plays without requiring the reading screen.

  Long-press on any word on a populated page opens the word sheet within
  one gesture; tafsir appears without leaving the page.

  Changing palette or night mode never resets the current page or the
  playback position.
