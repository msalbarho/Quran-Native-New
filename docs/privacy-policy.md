# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Quran application (“the App”) handles information. The App is a Quran reader and recitation player. It does not require an account or sign-in.

## Information the App does not require

The App does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, or phone number.

The developer does not use the App to show advertising and does not include developer analytics or tracking tools.

The App does not request access to the camera, microphone, contacts, or location.

## Permissions

The App uses Android permissions to support features such as:

- **Internet and network state**, so the App can stream recitation, load verse timing data, and tell whether a network is available.
- **Notifications**, so playback controls and status can be shown while audio is playing.
- **Foreground media playback, a wake lock, and a battery-optimization prompt**, so Quran audio can continue in the background, including with the screen off and with Android Auto or connected audio devices.
- **Storage on older Android versions only.** Current Android versions use scoped storage. The App does not need broad access to your files for ordinary use. On older versions, storage permission may be requested when saving a shared image or video.

## Quran audio, timing data, and downloads

Quran text, translations, and the fonts used to display the Mushaf are included in the App.

Recitations are streamed from external servers when you play them. Verse timing data, used to follow the recitation, may also be requested from an external server and stored on the device.

If you choose to download a recitation for offline listening, those audio files are saved in the App’s storage on your device. Downloads happen only when you start them.

## External services

Playback and timing requests are sent over the network to the content hosts configured in the App. These include **MP3Quran** and **QuranCDN**, and may include other Quran audio hosts used to deliver a selected recitation.

Those services are not operated by the developer of the App. A request can include ordinary technical information that any HTTPS connection carries, such as your IP address, and the address of the audio or timing file being requested. Their own privacy practices apply to that traffic.

## Information stored on your device

The App stores information locally so it can work without an account. This can include:

- settings and preferences, such as language, appearance, and reading position
- downloaded recitations and cached timing data
- bookmarks, memorization, and other data you create in the App
- playback position and related playback state
- a local note of the last fatal crash, kept on the device for diagnosis and not sent to the developer

If you use Android backup or device transfer, Android may include the App’s local databases, other than the packaged Quran text database, under Android’s own backup rules.

## Sharing

You can copy Quran text or share Quran text, images, or videos through Android’s share sheet. The App sends that content only to the app or service you choose. What that app or service does with it is governed by its own terms and privacy policy.

Shared images or videos may also be saved to your device’s gallery.

## Children

The App is suitable for a general audience, including children who read or listen to the Quran with a parent or guardian. The developer does not require an account and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Retention and deletion

The developer does not operate a user account or a developer-controlled database of App users.

Information the App stores remains on your device until it is removed. You can remove downloaded content or other local data through the App where that option is available. You can also clear the App’s data or uninstall the App in Android settings. Uninstalling removes the App’s local data from the device, except content you saved to shared storage, such as a gallery image or video.

## Security

Network requests for recitation and timing data use HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and the developer cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App’s practices change. The “Last updated” date above will be revised when that happens.
